📅 Last Updated: August 2024  |  Effective Immediately

1 Data We Collect

  • Location Optional GPS coordinates or manually selected city. Used solely for calculating accurate sunrise, sunset, and panchanga for your geographic position.
  • Profile Information Optional Name, address, and mobile number. Provided voluntarily. Used only to display on shared panchanga cards if you choose to share.
  • Device ID Anonymous A random UUID generated once on first launch. Cannot identify you personally. Used only for app usage statistics.
🔒 We do NOT collect emails, passwords, photos, contacts, call logs, SMS, or any sensitive personal information.

2 How We Use Your Data

  • Panchanga Calculations Computing Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Muhurta, Rahu Kala, and other Hindu calendar elements based on your location.
  • Shareable Panchanga Cards Generating daily panchanga images with your profile details (only if you provide them).
  • App Analytics Tracking anonymous usage (last seen, share count) to improve the app experience.

3 Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage Profile data and preferences are stored on your device using SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly sync.
  • Cloud Sync A copy of your profile is synced to Google Firebase Firestore (hosted in secure Google data centers) for backup purposes.
🛡️ All astronomical calculations (Swiss Ephemeris) run entirely on your device. No calculation data is sent to any server.

4 Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties.

Your data is used exclusively within the Bharatiyam Panchanga app for the purposes described above.

5 Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) Used for cloud data sync. Subject to Google's Terms of Service.
  • Swiss Ephemeris Open-source astronomical calculation library (AGPL-3.0). All computations run locally on-device. No data is sent to Astrodienst.

6 Permissions

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) For accurate panchanga based on your geographic position. You can deny this and select a city manually instead.
  • Internet (INTERNET) For Firebase cloud sync and checking for updates.
  • Storage For saving generated panchanga share images to your device.

7 Data Retention & Deletion

  • Uninstall Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Firebase Data To request deletion of your cloud-synced data, contact us at the email below. We will delete your data within 30 days.

8 Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
